`Signals` tells a client when the app regains focus or the device comes back online. Import it from the core entry point:

```ts
import { Signals } from "@monarcode/effetch-query";
```

Its exports follow, grouped by the guide that covers them. Each one has
the first paragraph of its docs, and what it needs and how it fails; your
editor shows the rest, with an example, when you hover the name.

## Focus and online signals [#focus-and-online-signals]

Guide: [Focus and online signals](/docs/client/signals).

### Signals.browser [#browser]

The browser's signals, the default: focus is the page becoming visible again, and online follows `navigator.onLine`. Without a DOM, such as on a server or in React Native, they never change.

### Signals.none [#none]

Signals that never change: no query refetches on focus or reconnect. The default is [`browser`](#browser).

### Signals.Signals [#signals]

Where a client hears that the app regained focus or the device came back online. Queries in use refetch on these when they're stale (the `refetchOnFocus` and `refetchOnReconnect` options).
